# Owl & Crayon

A math, art and reading app for kids, with an owl that answers questions in simple words. Works fully offline once opened.

- Source is in `src/` (`core.js` app, `math.js` curriculum, `art.js` art lessons, `english*.js` English & Reading: english.js and english4.js data, english2.js stories and grammar, english3.js and english5.js screens; english6.js (letter shapes, sound games, Talk time) and english7.js (chapter books) data with english8.js and english8.css the round-3 screens: Write letters, Sound games, Talk time, Chapter books, Grammar islands and the daily English mission; english9.js round-4 data (word families, listening pairs, the 44-sound wall, word power lists, 50 sayings and slang, 10 more Talk time chats) with english10.js and english10.css the round-4 screens: Sound lab, Word power and Read-along; `crafts.js` the Crafts corner's drawing kit, mini-movie engine and screens, with craft data for 115 crafts in 22 kinds in crafts2.js, crafts3.js and crafts4.js); `report.js` the Grown-ups corner's progress report). Run `python3 build.py` to regenerate `index.html` and `sw.js`.
- Source is in `src/` (`core.js` app, `math.js` curriculum, `art.js` art lessons, `english*.js` English & Reading: english.js and english4.js data, english2.js stories and grammar, english3.js and english5.js screens; `crafts.js` the Crafts corner's drawing kit, mini-movie engine and screens, with craft data for 158 crafts in 25 kinds in crafts2.js to crafts5.js; crafts6.js adds the round-3 movie kinds, the kid-paced player (finished-craft preview, slow motion, "I did this step ✓"), craft journeys with badges and printable templates, styled by crafts6.css); `report.js` the Grown-ups corner's progress report). Run `python3 build.py` to regenerate `index.html` and `sw.js`.
- Math source: `math.js` to `math4.js` the earlier topics; `math5.js` (with `math5.css`) 11 hands-on real-life topics (money, clocks, my day, measuring, temperature, pictograms, bar charts, sorting diagrams) and their widgets (set a clock by dragging its hands, pay with coins and notes, build a bar chart, put pictures in order, measure with blocks); `games.js` to `games4.js` the 23 math games (games4.js: Toy Shop and Clock Race). The Grown-ups corner's Settings pick the money: dollars and cents (default), pounds and pence, or euros and cents.
- Grown-ups corner (behind the times-table gate): a progress report per player covering Math, English & Reading, Art, Crafts, My World, Owl's Quest and play time, with a "This week" summary, 3 suggestions of what to try next, and a Print / save as PDF button. It is built only from what is saved on the device.
- Try it locally: `python3 -m http.server`, then open http://localhost:8000.
- Every push to `main` publishes to GitHub Pages. On the tablet, open the site once and use "Add to Home Screen".
